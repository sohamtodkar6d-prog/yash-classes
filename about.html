About Yash Classes

Founded and operated by Namrata Todkar, Yash Classes has 18 years of uninterrupted service in Wakad, Pune.

Namrata has 25+ years of teaching experience and has received teaching awards that recognize her commitment.

History

Before the COVID-19 lockdown, the institute ran two branches (Datta Mandir Road — ~200 students; Bhumkar Chowk — ~50 students) with a combined teaching staff of 15.

Philosophy

We focus on accessible and affordable education with a plan to expand our reach while keeping personal attention intact.
